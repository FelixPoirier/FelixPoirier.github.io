Homeflix privacy policy

Homeflix stores each profile's name, color, language choices, watchlist and watch history in a "Homeflix" folder in that person's own Google Drive, so the profile can be used on their other devices. The app can only access files it created itself; it cannot see anything else in your Drive. This data is not shared with anyone else.

To delete it, delete the Homeflix folder in your Google Drive and remove the app's access at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
